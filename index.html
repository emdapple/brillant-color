How it works

  1. Pick the choices. You can order 100, 200 or 300 cases. Demand will also be 100, 200 or 300, with the chances you entered.
  2. Work out the profit in each scenario. Profit = price × cases sold − cost × cases ordered. You can only sell what you ordered or what customers want, whichever is smaller: sold = min(order, demand).
  3. Weight by probability. Multiply each scenario's profit by its chance, then add them up. That is the expected profit of the order: what you'd earn per week on average if you made the same choice many weeks.
  4. Recommend the best. The order with the highest expected profit is highlighted. Expected profit is an average, so any single week can come out higher or lower.

If your chances don't add up to 100%, the app scales them so they do and shows a note.
